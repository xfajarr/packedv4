Every swap pays a fee. It doesn't go to a team — it accrues to holders, pro-rata by balance at the moment it lands, and settles into a fixed basket of tokenized equities. Your share is frozen when it accrues: selling never claws back what you already earned.

Mechanism

One loop, enforced by the hook

No multisig deciding who gets paid, no discretionary treasury, and no keeper that has to stay alive for you to get your money.

01

Trade

Every buy and sell pays a fee, taken in ETH on both sides.

02

Accrue

Split across holders instantly, weighted by balance at that moment.

03

Settle

The basket is bought once for many holders and split between them.

04

Receive

Every constituent lands in your own wallet. Nothing is held for you.

Anyone can settle on anyone's behalf. The assets always go to the holder; the caller takes a small tip for the gas. So an indexer can settle everyone, and no share is ever stranded because someone forgot to claim.

The difference

Small shares stay worth collecting

Make every holder buy their own basket and each one pays for a full set of swaps. A small holder's share ends up worth less than the gas to collect it, so it never gets collected. Buying the basket once for a whole batch collapses that cost.

What we checked, and how

Stock tokens
freely transferable Two third-party write-ups claimed whitelist-gated transfers. A simulated transfer from a real holder to an address that has never been onboarded succeeds.
PoolManager
0x8366a39C…0951 Three contracts on this chain are named PoolManager. v4 is a singleton, so the real one custodies every pool — 8,877 ETH. The other two hold zero ETH and only memecoins.
Token standard
ERC-20 + ERC-8056 A corporate-action multiplier moves shares-per-token on splits and dividends while raw balances stay put. Read it wrong and the basket breaks on the first split.
Opcode scan
clean No SELFDESTRUCT, no CALLCODE, in any of the three contracts. Scanned with a PUSH-aware parser, not a substring grep.
